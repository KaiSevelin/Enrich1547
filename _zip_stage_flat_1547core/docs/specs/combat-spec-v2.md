# Combat Spec V2

## Purpose

Combat is side-initiative based. One side acts first. During that side's activation
window, actors on that side may perform their available actions in flexible order.
Combat supports split action economy, pre-maneuver reservations, simultaneous attack
and defense resolution, reactions, post-maneuvers, and delayed RiskPoint
generation.

## Core Principles

1. Action economy is consumed on commit, not declaration.
2. Movement is a budget and can be split across multiple move declarations.
3. Multiple attacks may be interleaved with movement if attacks remain.
4. Pre-maneuver costs are reserved against a specific action context.
5. Reactions do not cancel attacks outright; they mutate or constrain them.
6. Reactions cannot trigger further reactions.
7. Crit points are generated by both attacker and defender.
8. Fumbles generate `RiskPoints`, which apply to the next single attack or
   defense roll in the same combat, then clear.
9. Post-maneuvers occur after damage-taken reactions.
10. Full-turn maneuvers do not trigger normal movement/attack reactions.

## Side Activation

Actors on the active side may act in flexible order. Movement can be split. Attacks
can be interleaved with movement. The side ends when a player on the active side
uses the `End Group Turn` control.

## Action Types

- `move`
- `attack`
- `full-turn-maneuver`

Pre-maneuvers are part of attack declaration, not their own separate action.

## Action Lifecycle

1. Declared
2. Validated
3. Reserved
4. Reaction window before resolution
5. Resolved
6. Reaction window after resolution
7. Committed
8. Cleanup

## Resource Rules

- Pre-maneuver costs are reserved at attack declaration.
- Reservations are bound to action contexts.
- Used reservations become spent on commit.
- Unused reservations release.
- Never globally spend all reserved points on an actor.

## Movement Rules

- Movement is square-based.
- Each move action resolves square by square.
- Combat range on the square grid uses Chebyshev distance by default.
- Distance between two squares is `max(abs(dx), abs(dy))`.
- Orthogonal and diagonal adjacency both count as distance `1`.
- Example: from `(x, y)`, the squares `(x+1, y)`, `(x, y+1)`, and `(x+1, y+1)`
  are all at distance `1`.
- Example: from `(x, y)`, the squares `(x+2, y+1)` and `(x+2, y+2)` are both at
  distance `2`.
- All combat reach and range checks use this metric unless a later rule
  explicitly overrides it.
- Threatened squares are determined by the actor's current facing and weapon
  reach.
- Example: an actor facing `N` with a Reach `1` weapon threatens `NW`, `N`, and
  `NE`.
- Example: an actor facing `N` with a Reach `2` weapon threatens 2 squares out
  on `NW`, `N`, and `NE`.
- Example: an actor facing `N` with a Reach `2-3` weapon threatens 3 squares out
  on `NW`, `N`, and `NE`, but not the squares immediately adjacent to the
  actor.
- That same facing creates a defense disadvantage against attacks from `SW`,
  `S`, and `SE`.
- Entering a threatened square commits movement into that square.
- Reaction resolves from that square.
- If movement is interrupted, only moved squares commit.

## Attack Rules

Attack payload minimum:

```js
{
  attackDice: [],
  targets: [],
  attackType
}
```

Attack flow:

1. Attack declared
2. Pre-maneuvers attached
3. Costs reserved
4. Reaction window opens
5. Reactions mutate attack or defense
6. Attack and defense rolls resolve simultaneously
7. Damage applies
8. Damage-taken reactions resolve
9. Post-maneuver window opens
10. Defender spends post-maneuvers first
11. Attacker spends post-maneuvers second
12. Crit points clear
13. Reservations spend/release
14. Action commits

## Safe Attack

A safe attack is a normal attack except:

- No crits are generated
- No fumbles are generated
- No reactions are generated from that attack

It has no other inherent penalty or bonus. If the maneuver that initiated the safe
attack also modifies dice or totals, those changes still apply.

## Crit and Fumble Rules

- Both attacker and defender may generate crits.
- Both attacker and defender may generate fumbles.
- Fumbles on a roll generate `RiskPoints`.
- `RiskPoints` are stored on actor state.
- `RiskPoints` become risk dice on the next single attack or defense roll in the
  same combat.
- After that roll, `RiskPoints` are set to `0`.
- When combat ends, any remaining `RiskPoints` are set to `0`.

## Full-Turn Maneuvers

- Consume normal move and attack availability on commit.
- Last until that same side becomes active again.
- May create persistent effects such as Overwatch.

## Group Targeting

- Targets are always an array.
- Usually there is one target.
- Group target acquisition should ideally use Foundry templates.

## Reaction Rules

- Reaction maneuvers can only be used once per turn per maneuver type.
- Reactions do not trigger further reactions.
- Reaction-generated attacks or effects must be marked as non-reactive.
